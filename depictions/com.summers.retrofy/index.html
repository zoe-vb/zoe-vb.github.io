Retrofy

Classic Spotify. Modern playback. Legacy iOS.

About

Retrofy brings Spotify back to legacy iOS devices, with an interface inspired by the classic 2012 Spotify app.

Designed for iOS 6 and ARMv7 devices, Retrofy uses librespot to provide modern Spotify playback functionality on older hardware.

Using an older device?

If you're running iOS 4 or 5, or your device does not support ARMv7, install Retrofy4 instead.

Features

  • Full Spotify track playback with Premium, powered by librespot
  • Browse your playlists with artwork, Liked Songs and search
  • Now Playing with seeking, shuffle, repeat and automatic track progression
  • Like tracks and add them to playlists
  • Lock screen playback controls and Control Center integration where supported
  • Resume where you left off after relaunching

Requirements

  • iOS: 6.0 or later
  • Processor: ARMv7 (iPhone 3GS or compatible device)
  • Account: Spotify Premium for full tracks; otherwise, 30-second previews
