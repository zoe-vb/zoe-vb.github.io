About

Retrofy is a Spotify client for iOS 6, styled like the 2012 Spotify app.

Features

  • Full track playback with Spotify Premium, through librespot
  • Your playlists with covers, Liked Songs and search
  • Now Playing with a seek bar, shuffle, repeat and auto next
  • Like songs and add them to playlists
  • Lock screen and Control Center controls
  • Picks up where you left off after relaunching

Requirements

  • iOS 6 or later
  • iPhone 5 or another armv7s device
  • Spotify Premium for full tracks, otherwise 30 second previews
