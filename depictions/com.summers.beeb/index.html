About

Beeb is a lightweight BBC iPlayer client designed specifically for iOS 6.

Features

  • Stream live TV and catch-up programs
  • Optimized interface for vintage iOS design languages
  • AirPlay support for streaming to Apple TV
  • Browse by categories, channels, and schedules
  • Save your favorite shows for quick access
  • Picks up playback where you left off

Requirements

  • iOS 6.0 or later
  • iPhone 4S, iPhone 5, or compatible armv7/armv7s device
  • Active internet connection (Wi-Fi or Cellular)
