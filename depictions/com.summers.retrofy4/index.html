About

Retrofy4 is a Spotify client for iOS 4 and later, styled after the classic 2012 Spotify app.

This variant is designed for older iOS versions and devices, with ARMv6 support.

Which version should I use?
  • iOS 4 or 5: Use Retrofy4.
  • iOS 6, iPhone 4 or newer: Use the standard Retrofy.
  • iOS 6, iPhone 3GS: Use Retrofy4.

Features

  • Full track playback with Spotify Premium, powered by librespot
  • Playlists with covers, Liked Songs and search
  • Now Playing with a seek bar, shuffle, repeat and automatic next track
  • Like songs and add them to playlists
  • Lock screen playback controls (where supported)
  • Resume where you left off after relaunching

Requirements

  • iOS 4.2.1 or later
  • ARMv6 or compatible ARMv7 processor
  • Spotify Premium for full tracks; otherwise, 30-second previews
